Each appears only where your Meet server has [set it up](features-controls.md#set-up-by-your-administrator). The owner and co-hosts can start a recording or a transcription, and often signed-in participants too, see [Permissions](features-controls.md#permissions). Anyone else can click **Request**.

## Recording

Saves a video of the meeting. Open **More tools** → **Record** and click **Start recording the meeting**. A red indicator shows everyone it is running.

- It starts once someone's microphone, camera or screen share is on.
- It can stop on its own, at a time limit your Meet server sets.
- The download link goes by email to whoever started it and the hosts. Only they can open it, and it may expire after some days.
- No email? Ask your administrator, who can find the recording.

## Transcription *(beta)* { #transcription }

Turns what people say into a document in [LaSuite Docs](https://github.com/suitenumerique/docs), with a summary where your Meet server writes one. Open **More tools** → **Transcribe**, check the meeting language, since the right one gives a better transcript, and click **Start transcribing the meeting**. Tick **Also start a video recording** to get a video too.

## Real-time subtitles *(beta)* { #real-time-subtitles }

Shows what people say as live captions, which are not kept. Anyone can click **Show subtitles** in the control bar. Their size and colours are set in [Accessibility & Shortcuts](accessibility.md#display).
