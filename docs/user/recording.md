# Recording & Transcription

Get a video or a written record of a meeting, or follow it with live captions, as its host or as a participant.

<figure markdown="span">
  ![More tools panel](../assets/recording.gif)
  <figcaption>More tools → Record or Transcribe</figcaption>
</figure>

Each appears only where your Meet server has [set it up](features-controls.md#set-up-by-your-administrator).

[Permissions](features-controls.md#permissions) lists who can start a recording or a transcription, and anyone else can click **Request**.

## Recording

Saves a video of the meeting.

1. Open **More tools** → **Record**.
2. Click **Start recording the meeting**.

A red indicator shows everyone it is running.

- It starts once someone's microphone, camera or screen share is on.
- It can stop on its own, at a time limit your Meet server sets.
- The download link goes by email to whoever started it, the host and the co-hosts. Only they can open it, and it may expire after some days.
- No email? Ask your administrator, who can find the recording.

## Transcription *(beta)* { #transcription }

Turns what people say into a document in [LaSuite Docs](https://github.com/suitenumerique/docs), with a summary where your Meet server writes one.

1. Open **More tools** → **Transcribe**.
2. Check the meeting language: the right one gives a better transcript.
3. To get a video too, tick **Also start a video recording**.
4. Click **Start transcribing the meeting**.

## Real-time subtitles *(beta)* { #real-time-subtitles }

Shows what people say as live captions, which are not kept. Anyone can click **Show subtitles** in the control bar.

Their size and colours are set as [Live subtitles](guides/deaf-hard-of-hearing.md#live-subtitles) shows.
