# Recording & Transcription

- **Recording** saves a video of the meeting.
- **Transcription** turns what people say into text, and writes a summary of it where your instance does.
- **Real-time subtitles** show what people say as captions, live.

  <figcaption>More tools → Record or Transcribe</figcaption>
</figure>

| | Recording | Transcription *(beta)* | Real-time subtitles *(beta)* |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Start it from** | **More tools** → **Record** | **More tools** → **Transcribe** | **Show subtitles** in the control bar |
| **Who can start it** | The owner and co-hosts, often signed-in participants too, see [Permissions](features-controls.md#permissions). Others can **Request** it | The same as recording | Anyone |
| **The result** | A download link by email, to whoever started it and the hosts | A document in LaSuite Docs | Not kept |

Each appears only where your instance has [set it up](features-controls.md#set-up-by-your-administrator).

## Recording

Click **Start recording the meeting**. A red indicator shows everyone it is running.

- It starts once someone's microphone, camera or screen share is on.
- It can stop on its own, at a time limit your instance sets.
- Only the people who got the email can open the download link, and it may expire after some days.
- No email? Ask your administrator, who can find the recording.

## Transcription

Check the meeting language, since the right one gives a better transcript, then click **Start transcribing the meeting**. Tick **Also start a video recording** to get a video too.

Your instance needs LaSuite Docs: without it, nobody receives the transcript.

## Real-time subtitles

Their size and colours are set in [Accessibility & Shortcuts](accessibility.md#display).
