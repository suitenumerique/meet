# Accessibility & Shortcuts

Meet works with a keyboard alone and with screen readers. It follows the recommendations of [RGAA](https://accessibilite.numerique.gouv.fr/), France's accessibility standard, but has not been audited yet.

Each need has its own guide, with the settings and shortcuts that matter for it:

| Guide | For |
|---|---|
| [Hosting a large meeting](guides/large-meeting-host.md) | Teachers and speakers |
| [On a phone or a weak connection](guides/phone-or-weak-connection.md) | Phones and poor networks |
| [Deaf or hard of hearing](guides/deaf-hard-of-hearing.md) | Following by reading |
| [Blind or low vision](guides/blind-low-vision.md) | Screen readers, keyboard, magnifiers |
| [Sign-language interpreter](guides/sign-language-interpreter.md) | Interpreters and their hosts |

## Keyboard

The usual keys work everywhere: `Tab`, the arrow keys, `Enter` and `Escape`. A **skip link** at the top of the page jumps to the main content.

In a meeting, `Ctrl+Shift+/` or **Settings → Shortcuts** lists these shortcuts:

| Action | Shortcut |
|---|---|
| Focus the bottom toolbar | `F2` |
| Focus the page buttons, when participants fill more than one page | `Ctrl+Shift+G` |
| Microphone on or off | `Ctrl+D` |
| Camera on or off | `Ctrl+E` |
| Talk while muted | Hold `V` |
| Reactions | `Ctrl+Shift+E` |
| Fullscreen | `Ctrl+Shift+F` |
| Recording panel | `Ctrl+Shift+L` |
| Raise or lower your hand | `Ctrl+Shift+H` |
| Chat | `Ctrl+Shift+M` |
| Participants panel | `Ctrl+Shift+P` |
| Zoom in or out on a shared screen, while it has focus | `+`, `-` |
| Fit a zoomed shared screen to the window | `0` |
| Move around a zoomed shared screen | Arrow keys |

On a Mac, `Ctrl` is `⌘`.

## Reporting a problem

Open an [issue on GitHub](https://github.com/suitenumerique/meet/issues/new?assignees=&labels=bug&template=Bug_report.md) with "accessibility" in its title. Give your browser, your assistive technology and its version, and the steps that lead to the problem.
